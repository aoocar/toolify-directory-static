Back-to-school is when information gaps are the widest and freshmen pay the most "tuition." This list does not give you a "must-buy" checklist — it groups the **traps by category** and points out the 10 most common ones, each linked to an indexed Dawn Island creator that can actually help.

## Gear traps: do not stockpile what you will not use

**Trap 1 — Hoarding "dorm magic products" before moving in.** Following the hype and buying storage boxes, bedding, and makeover gear before you know your dorm conditions and habits. The right move: move in first, restock by real need, and only buy what you will actually use in the first week (power strips, bedding, storage, desk lamp).

> See [Lazy Classmate's Dorm](/en/accounts/lan-tongxue-de-sushe) (tested goods) and [Organizer Da Mei](/en/accounts/shouna-zhenglishi-damei) (plan before you buy).

**Trap 2 — Chasing laptop specs instead of your needs.** Buying a "value gaming laptop" and then hauling a 5-pound machine to class. Pick by your major's real needs — coding, video editing, and design have completely different requirements.

> See [Bibar Pingceshi](/en/accounts/bibarpingceshi) (measured data) and [Zhongzheng Pingce](/en/accounts/zhongzheng-pingce) (self-funded, ad-free) for buying logic.

## Information traps: verify anything that pressures you to pay

**Trap 3 — "On-the-spot senior discounts."** Data plans, gym memberships, driving schools, English training… anything that makes you scan-and-pay on the spot with "last day of the deal" — check official prices and reviews first. Truly valuable info does not need pressure selling.

**Trap 4 — "Guaranteed award" contests and certificates.** "Pay for a guaranteed award" or "certificate expires in a year" — verify whether the contest is officially organized, whether the certificate is useful, and what the bar and risks are. If it cannot be explained clearly, set it aside.

> See [I Love Competitions](/en/accounts/woaijingsai-wang) (verifying contest sources) and [Shixiseng](/en/accounts/shixiseng-wang) (judging job-post authenticity).

**Trap 5 — Gigs that ask for a deposit first.** Online gigs requiring deposits, click-farming rebates, "typing jobs paid daily"… legitimate gigs never ask you to pay first. Anything that takes your money before the work — skip it.

## Academic traps: GPA and CET-4/6 matter from day one

**Trap 6 — Believing "college is easy."** GPA directly affects bao-yan, scholarships, major transfers, and job resumes. The gap opens in year one and is hardest to fix later. Do not treat "work hard in high school, relax in college" as truth.

> See [Song Hao](/en/accounts/songhao-laoshi) (systematic college-math courses) and [Labi & Xiaoxun](/en/accounts/labi-he-xiaoxun) (learning science).

**Trap 7 — Putting off CET-4/6.** Your high-school English vocabulary has a shelf life. Pass CET-4/6 early in year one instead of juggling it with internships and other prep later.

> See [Kaochong CET-4/6](/en/accounts/kaochong-siliuji) (companion-style prep).

## Planning traps: decide your goal, then work backwards

**Trap 8 — Joining every club, internship, and contest.** Wanting to do everything means you do nothing deeply. Decide whether you want bao-yan, kaoyan, or a job first, then work backwards to the experiences you need and go deep in 1-2 directions.

**Trap 9 — Trusting "veteran" paths without verification.** Other people's paths carry their era and school context; they may not fit you. Reference is fine, but decide on the basis of official information and your own situation.

> See [Zhang Xuefeng](/en/accounts/zhangxuefeng-laoshi) (major and grad-school planning) — use its analytical framework, then weigh it against yourself.

**Trap 10 — Underestimating the value of information.** Many opportunities are not missing — you just do not know they exist. Regularly checking contest, internship, and bao-yan/kaoyan sources saves you many detours.

## One-line summary

There is one rule to judge any "trap": **if it pressures you to pay now without explaining the bar and risks — set it aside.** Anything truly worth your investment can survive careful verification.
