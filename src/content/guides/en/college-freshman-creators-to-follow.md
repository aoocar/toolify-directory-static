One of the biggest freshman traps is following a bunch of accounts that look lively but do not actually help. This guide does not rank by followers — it groups creators **by your back-to-school scenario**, so you follow the ones that fill the gap you have right now.

All 10 creators mentioned are indexed on Dawn Island; click a name to see the platform, content focus, and verified data.

## Buying a laptop: the measured-data school

### [Bibar Pingceshi](/en/accounts/bibarpingceshi)

A veteran Bilibili laptop-review channel known for measured data, chassis comparisons, and cooling tests — long a go-to for students and computer beginners.

**Who it suits**: freshmen buying their first laptop who want real test data rather than vendor marketing.
**How to use it**: start with its buying-guide series to build your judgment standard, then look up the specific models on your shortlist.

### [Zhongzheng Pingce](/en/accounts/zhongzheng-pingce)

A self-funded, ad-free reviewer of laptops and desktops, known for teardown tests and value-first comparisons — well regarded among budget-conscious students.

**Who it suits**: freshmen on a budget who want the best pick within their limit.
**How to use it**: treat its reviews as your budget guard — set the line first, then verify value with its comparisons.

### [Xihuan Jiu Mai](/en/accounts/xihuan-jiu-mai)

A digital-buying creator whose principle is "buy rationally, buy what you need", covering laptop peripherals, bags, and accessories.

**Who it suits**: impulse buyers who want every yuan to land where it counts.
**How to use it**: check it before buying any accessory or bag — it is built to tell you whether something is actually worth buying.

## Understanding the machine: the science school

### [Yingjian Chatan](/en/accounts/yingjian-chatan)

Famous for animation-based explanations of PC hardware — CPUs, GPUs, motherboards, and the full build process — approachable and easy to follow.

**Who it suits**: freshmen with zero hardware background who want to know what is actually inside their laptop.
**How to use it**: watch the principle animations to build bottom-up knowledge; then spec sheets and reviews stop being intimidating.

## Cross-category digital round-ups: principle + data

### [Geekerwan](/en/accounts/geekerwan)

A hardcore tech-review team known for engineering-level teardowns and data visualization, explaining the logic behind chips and devices.

**Who it suits**: freshmen who want to build their own judgment standard instead of being led by specs.
**How to use it**: use it to understand "why it is good" and build your baseline.

### [Xiaobai Pingce](/en/accounts/xiaobai-pingce)

A reviewer known for teardowns and data comparison, with same-room round-ups giving comparable objective numbers.

**Who it suits**: freshmen torn between a few models who need comparable data to decide.
**How to use it**: in the final shortlist stage, use its same-room comparisons to make the call.

## Dorm goods & peripherals: the ad-free school

### [GuDe Hot List](/en/accounts/gude-rebang)

An ad-free reviewer in the home-appliance and digital space, covering everything from appliances to student-grade gear.

**Who it suits**: freshmen buying dorm goods and peripherals who are tired of being marketed at.
**How to use it**: before buying any "back-to-school miracle" product, search it first to see whether it is actually worth it.

## After you get the laptop: turning gear into productivity

### [oeasy](/en/accounts/oeasy)

A "versatile" free teacher on Bilibili offering complete, from-zero Office and computer tutorials.

**Who it suits**: freshmen who still only "double-click" Office and want to actually use their laptop.
**How to use it**: follow the systematic courses to build solid document, spreadsheet, and presentation basics — you will use them all four years.

### [GenJi Is Really Trying to Teach You](/en/accounts/genji-shizhen-ai)

An AI-application teaching creator who turns AI tools into concrete, hands-on skills.

**Who it suits**: freshmen who want AI to become a study and creation productivity tool.
**How to use it**: learn AI skills on demand — essays, slides, and research all become much faster.

## Inspiring campus life: creative tech

### [He Tongxue](/en/accounts/he-tongxue)

A top tech creator on Bilibili known for creative experiments that connect technology with campus life and human curiosity.

**Who it suits**: freshmen who want to stay curious and inspired about technology.
**How to use it**: treat it as interest fuel — keep your excitement alive, then go back to hardcore content for depth.

## Three tests: is it worth following long-term?

**One — does it clarify scenarios and premises?** A recommendation that only quotes specs without saying "who it suits, under what conditions" is low-value.

**Two — does it separate measured review from subjective opinion?** Channels that split objective data from personal views are more trustworthy.

**Three — does it update systematically?** Digital hardware refreshes every year; only channels that keep pace and update in a system are worth following long-term.
