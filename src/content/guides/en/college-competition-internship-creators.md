The fastest way to lose in college is not through lack of effort but through **information gaps** — not knowing which competitions, internships, and paths are worth your time. This guide does not rank by followers; it groups creators **by your goal**: whatever you want right now, follow the creators that fit.

All 10 creators mentioned are indexed on Dawn Island; click a name to see the platform, content focus, and verified data.

## Math modeling: one of the most rewarding discipline contests

### [Mathematical Modeling LaoGe](/en/accounts/shuxue-jianmo-laoge)

A leading math-modeling coach covering the full competition workflow — topic selection, modeling methods, paper writing, and real-exam breakdowns for CUMCM and MCM.

**Who it suits**: contest newcomers who want systematic guidance.
**How to use it**: treat it as a coach — run through topic selection to paper writing with its methods instead of scattered video-watching.

### [Math Modeling BOOM](/en/accounts/shuxue-jianmo-boom)

Practical prep — common algorithms, model tutorials, real-exam walkthroughs — with a Q&A community for stuck moments.

**Who it suits**: freshmen who want a prep path and peers to ask.
**How to use it**: treat it as a prep workbook — learn the algorithms first, then use the community to unblock questions.

### [Math Modeling Study Exchange](/en/accounts/shuxue-jianmo-xuexijiaoliu)

A long-running modeling community with tutorials, algorithms, contest insights, and a deep resource archive.

**Who it suits**: students who want a stable, systematic learning source.
**How to use it**: treat it as a resource library — learn the system, then tackle real exams to build a complete modeling framework.

## Math contests: follow the right method to place

### [Math Contest Brother Fei](/en/accounts/shuxue-jingsai-feige)

Specializes in contests like the National College Mathematics Competition — real-exam breakdowns and method summaries that turn hard problems into repeatable approaches.

**Who it suits**: students aiming to place in math contests.
**How to use it**: treat it as a method coach — summarize approaches, then practice real exams to turn "I can't" into "I know where to start."

## Contest & opportunity news: know what is out there first

### [I Love Competitions](/en/accounts/woaijingsai-wang)

A contest-and-opportunity bulletin aggregating competitions, volunteer recruitment, and youth programs nationwide.

**Who it suits**: freshmen who want to catch every worthwhile opportunity.
**How to use it**: treat it as an opportunity radar — check periodically for open contests, then filter by your goal.

## Internship info: learn the recruiting rhythm early

### [Shixiseng](/en/accounts/shixiseng-wang)

The official Bilibili account of a well-known internship platform — internship info, resume and interview tips, and career-growth content.

**Who it suits**: students who want an early look at the internship market.
**How to use it**: treat it as career onboarding — learn resumes and interviews first, then start applying.

### [Duding Campus Recruiting](/en/accounts/duding-xiaozhao)

Aggregates campus-recruiting and autumn-recruiting postings plus summer/winter internships.

**Who it suits**: students who want to plan the recruiting calendar and never miss a window.
**How to use it**: treat it as a recruiting calendar — track autumn-recruiting and internship timelines from year one.

## Industry awareness: see the road before you choose it

### [Smart Workplace Observer](/en/accounts/jizhi-zhichang-guancha)

Explains what different industries and roles actually look like day to day, helping students build career awareness.

**Who it suits**: freshmen exploring career directions.
**How to use it**: treat it as an industry map — see what roles really look like before choosing a major or internship.

## Grad-school planning: the long road for bao-yan and kaoyan

### [Zhong BaoYan Dao](/en/accounts/zhongbaoyan-dao)

Guides the full bao-yan pathway — eligibility, GPA and contest strategy, school selection, and interview prep.

**Who it suits**: freshmen planning graduate school from day one.
**How to use it**: treat it as a bao-yan playbook — learn eligibility requirements early and work backwards to GPA, contests, and research.

### [Yantu Kaoyan Education](/en/accounts/yantu-kaoyan)

The official account of Yantu Education offering full kaoyan courses — politics, English, math, and school planning.

**Who it suits**: undergraduates committing to the graduate entrance exam.
**How to use it**: treat it as a course timetable — choose subjects by your foundation and prepare at a steady rhythm.

## Three tests: is it worth following long-term?

**One — are the sources verified?** Competition and internship info suffers most from hearsay; beware of channels that push "insider channels" without naming official sources.

**Two — does it clarify who it suits and the bar to entry?** Channels that only shout "guaranteed award" or "easy win" without explaining the bar and risks are low-value.

**Three — does it update consistently?** Contest calendars, recruiting windows, and bao-yan policies keep changing; only channels that update systematically are worth following long-term.
