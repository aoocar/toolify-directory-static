Office software is the most-used yet most-underrated workplace skill. Plenty of people have used Excel for a decade and still sum by hand; plenty build slides by swapping templates and watch them break the moment a requirement changes.

This guide does not rank by follower count. It groups creators by **the specific skill you are missing**. Find your gap and follow the right creator.

All ten creators below have profiles indexed on Dawn Island; click a name to see the platform, content focus, and any verified public figures.

## Want a complete Office foundation

### [oeasy](/en/accounts/oeasy)

A "do-everything" free educator on Bilibili covering Photoshop, Premiere, Office, audio editing, and more — known for starting from absolute zero, hand-holding through every step, and holding nothing back. The tutorial series is exceptionally complete.

**Who it suits**: true beginners who want to lay a solid Office foundation in one pass.
**How to use it**: do not pick and choose. Watch his series in order to build the underlying logic of each tool first; return later for specific needs.

## PPT design and aesthetics

### [Pangmen PPT](/en/accounts/pangmen-ppt)

A leading PPT design educator on Bilibili, focused on layout principles, visual logic, and taste training rather than simply stacking templates.

**Who it suits**: people who build reports, pitches, and courseware and want them clear and good-looking, not merely acceptable.
**How to use it**: study his "why this layout" series to build layout judgement instead of copying formats.

### [Qiuye PPT](/en/accounts/qiuye-ppt)

A workplace-oriented PPT training brand with strong template thinking, quick-win techniques, and high-frequency scenarios — very practical.

**Who it suits**: time-pressed professionals who must deliver a passable deck fast.
**How to use it**: treat it as a first-aid kit. Use its quick routines under a deadline; use Pangmen to build taste the rest of the time.

## Excel and WPS hands-on

### [Excel Caozuo](/en/accounts/excel-caozuo)

A Douyin channel for practical Excel — functions, pivot tables, and everyday high-frequency operations, explained directly and ready to apply.

**Who it suits**: anyone living in spreadsheets but feeling stuck at basic operations.
**How to use it**: search his coverage of a specific pain point (vlookup, dedupe, split columns) — more reliable than scattered search-engine answers.

### [WPS Dalaoban](/en/accounts/wps-dalaoban)

A Douyin educator in the WPS ecosystem — words, sheets, and presentations in the domestic office suite, close to real Chinese workplace use.

**Who it suits**: people whose company runs WPS rather than Office and need locally relevant features.
**How to use it**: for WPS-specific functions (document collaboration, template centre) search him first — more on-point than generic Office tutorials.

## Bite-sized workplace tips (Douyin)

The five creators below are all Douyin workplace-efficiency accounts with short, punchy styles — good for commute-time brushing, covering shortcuts, template ideas, and small tricks.

### [Chenge Bangong](/en/accounts/chenge-bangong)

Covers Word, Excel, and PPT workplace tips with a fast pace and clear scenarios.

### [Duidui Bangong](/en/accounts/duidui-bangong)

Approaches efficiency from a "debunk the playbook" angle, often dismantling inefficient habits — good for anyone wanting to change how they work.

### [Houge Bangong](/en/accounts/houge-bangong)

Leans toward Excel and data-handling in real office scenarios.

### [Bingbing jie](/en/accounts/bingbing-jie)

Covers multi-tool workplace efficiency and communication, light and easy to pick up.

### [Sanmiao Office](/en/accounts/sanmiao-office)

Ultra-short trick snapshots — good for "learn one move while waiting for the elevator".

**How to use these five**: treat them as inspiration and a quick-reference, not as a course. Save useful moves and drill them over the weekend — far better than forgetting them the same day.

## How to judge whether an office-teaching channel is worth following long-term

Three workable tests:

**Is it systematic?** Accounts posting only scattered shortcuts save you seconds but do not grow your skill. Series with a through-line are worth the long follow.

**Does it explain the principle?** "Click here and it works" loses to "why you click here". The first leaves you helpless in a new scenario; the second lets you generalise.

**Does it match your version?** Office and WPS differ a lot in UI and version. A channel still teaching the 2007 menu helps little for someone on the latest WPS.

## Common mistakes

- **Collecting but not practising.** Office software is muscle memory — watching ten times beats nothing; doing it once beats watching ten times.
- **Chasing new tools blindly.** Master pivots and slide masters first, then consider efficiency plugins, or you invert means and ends.
- **Relying entirely on templates.** Templates are fine for emergencies, but long-term dependence erodes your independent layout ability — and you stall the moment a non-standard request arrives.
