## Who is this
Yang Sheng Tang is Beijing TV's health program running for over a decade; its Douyin account condenses expert-led TCM, nutrition, and disease-prevention episodes into short clips, ~16.2M followers.

## Content style
Doctors explain on camera and the TV-grade expertise is cut into practical, swipeable science that stresses "understandable and usable".

## Who it's for
Older users tracking wellness and chronic-care basics; adult children curating health tips for parents; creators in the senior-health space.
