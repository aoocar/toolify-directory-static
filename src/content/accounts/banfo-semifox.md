## 内容特点
把公司运作、割韭菜套路、市场规律裹进段子与梗里，嬉笑怒骂背后是扎实调研，硬核但不枯燥。

## 适合谁看
想搞懂商业与金融逻辑的普通人；对互联网公司与行业八卦有兴趣的读者；厌倦枯燥财经报道、要"听得进去"的人。

## 从哪期入门

## Who is this
Hardcore Semi-Fox Immortal (Banfo) is a top Bilibili explainer in business and finance, using absurdist memes and rapid delivery since 2019, ~7.73M followers.

## Content style
It wraps how companies, scams, and markets actually work in jokes and memes; behind the comedy is real research—hardcore but never dull.

## Who it's for
Ordinary readers who want business and finance made clear; people curious about internet companies and industry gossip; anyone tired of dry financial news.

## Where to start
Pick a company or industry you already know—his videos are mostly standalone, so any one is a fine entry point.
