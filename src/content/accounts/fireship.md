## 适合谁看
程序员与独立开发者；想快速建立技术概念图谱的初学者；关注 AI 工程化的读者。

## 从哪期入门

## Who is this
Fireship is one of YouTube's most popular developer channels, famous for "100 seconds" explainers and rapid-fire tutorials since 2017, ~3.2M subscribers, covering AI tools, LLMs, web frameworks, and programming languages.

## Content style
Very short, humorous clips make complex developer topics fun and accessible—concept speed-runs, not long derivations.

## Who it's for
Programmers and indie devs; beginners who want a fast concept map; readers following applied AI.

## Where to start
Search any "100 Seconds" video—consistent pacing, each clip self-contained, great for building an overview in spare minutes.
