## 他是谁
3Blue1Brown 是 Grant Sanderson 创建的 YouTube 数学科普频道，自 2015 年起以自研动画引擎 manim 制作内容，订阅约 650 万。

## 内容特点
用动画把线性代数、微积分、神经网络等抽象主题可视化，先建立直觉再讲推导，避开公式堆砌。其神经网络系列被许多学习者视为深度学习的最佳入门之一。

## 适合谁看
想补数学直觉的学习者；准备入门 AI 与深度学习、希望搞懂"为什么"的人；具备高中数学底子、偏好可视化讲解的读者。

## 从哪期入门
①《神经网络》系列 ②《线性代数本质》 ③《微积分本质》。建议先看完"本质"系列打底，再进入网络专题。

## Who is this
3Blue1Brown is a YouTube channel created by Grant Sanderson that visualizes mathematics with a custom animation engine called manim. It has around 6.5M subscribers and has published since 2015.

## Content style
Complex topics—linear algebra, calculus, neural networks—are explained by building visual intuition first, then derivation. The "Essence of" series and the neural-network videos are often cited as the gentlest entry points into deep learning.

## Who it's for
Learners who want intuition, not just formulas; people starting in AI/ML who need the "why"; anyone comfortable with high-school math who prefers visual explanations.

## Where to start
① The Neural Networks series ② Essence of Linear Algebra ③ Essence of Calculus. Watch the "Essence" set first, then the network deep-dive.
